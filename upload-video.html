Video File Analysis

Upload pre-recorded footage to detect helmet violations with multi-frame burst plate consensus.

Select or Drag Video File Supported: MP4, AVI, MOV (Max 100MB)
How It Works
  1. Upload a recorded traffic video (MP4/AVI/MOV).
  2. Frames are sampled at intervals matching the livestream detection stride.
  3. Riders & motorcycles are paired to identify helmet violations.
  4. Multi-frame burst consensus extracts sharp license plate crops.
  5. Focused motorcycle ROI evidence snapshots and reports are committed to database.
Helmet Classes:
  • Compliant Proper helmet (no violation)
  • Non-Compliant No helmet or improper nutshell (violation)
Note: MotoWatch is for monitoring and violation detection — not primary enforcement.
